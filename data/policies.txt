REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Refund eligibility must be verified before a refund is approved or processed. This application can explain policy information but cannot approve or issue a refund.

SHIPPING POLICY

Standard delivery generally takes 3–5 business days. Delivery estimates may change because of carrier delays. Customers can check their order status using their order ID.

CANCELLATION POLICY

Cancellation requests must be checked against the current order status. An order that has already shipped may not be eligible for cancellation.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Customers should contact support with their order ID and a brief description of the issue. Eligibility depends on the order details and the applicable policy. Do not promise an outcome unless the available policy and order information support it.
